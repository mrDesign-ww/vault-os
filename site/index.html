How it works

A brain, a pair of hands, and a loop between them.

Obsidian holds the knowledge. Claude Code does the work. An evolution loop keeps the whole thing tuned.

You ask. the hands act the brain records the loop refines the next ask starts smarter.

Automation · hooks

It works while you work.

Four hooks fire on their own across a session, so the vault stays saved, loaded, and current without a single manual step.

session start SessionStart Loads your recent context (hot cache) — Claude resumes where you left off.
on write / edit PostToolUse Auto-commits every vault change to git — nothing is ever lost.
session end Stop Nudges you to refresh the hot cache and capture new knowledge.
when due Health nudge Suggests running evolution when the vault is due for a tidy-up.
quietly

The system works in the background so you don't have to. No commands to remember, nothing to save by hand.

Stays sharp · evolution

It keeps itself sharp.

A loop tidies the vault, learns from friction, and turns your habits into tools. Every change is a proposal you approve — never a silent edit.

proposes → you approve → applies
  • 1

    Detect. A Curator and a reflection pass scan memory, the wiki, and your session history.

  • 2

    Propose. It drafts fixes — consolidations, fresher retrieval, even new skills — and shows them.

  • 3

    Approve. You review the diff. Nothing lands without your yes (safe mechanical fixes aside).

  • 4

    Apply. Approved changes commit, the index re-syncs, and the loop comes back around.
